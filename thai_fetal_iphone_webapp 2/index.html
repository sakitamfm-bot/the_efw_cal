Categories describe position on the reference only. SGA/FGR is classified in the Doppler & FGR card using the Delphi consensus criteria and ISUOG 2020 guidance. This is a decision-support aid, not a substitute for clinical assessment.

Pooled Thai reference (default)

Every Thai source whose data could be obtained is combined. Each source counts once (one hospital ≈ one region). For each week, the pooled distribution is the equal mixture of the source distributions: mean = average of the source means; variance = average of the source variances + the variance between the source means, so differences between centres are part of the normal range. The weekly values are then smoothed with weighted least squares (cubic mean, quadratic SD, weights = number of fetuses that week), the same approach the Siriraj papers used. Centile = mean + z·SD.

  • BPD, AC, FL (14–41 wk): Siriraj 2000 (central, n≈610, cross-sectional) + Khon Kaen 2007 (northeast, n=628, cross-sectional) + Sukhothai 2007 (north, 34/wk, longitudinal).
  • HC (14–41 wk): Khon Kaen 2007 + Sukhothai 2007. The Siriraj HC paper (Sutanthavibul 2000, Siriraj Hosp Gaz 52:445–51) could not be obtained. At 41 wk only Khon Kaen contributes; the smoothed median is 11 mm below its single 41-wk value.
  • EFW (24–42 wk): Chiang Mai 1993 + Rajavithi 2014 birthweight charts. Chulalongkorn 1984 (P10/50/90 from 28 wk) was checked but not pooled: it predates routine ultrasound dating and sits 0.1–0.8 SD below the pool.
  • Validation: in every parameter, each source's weekly means fall inside the pooled 10th–90th centile in 96–100% of weeks. Leaving any one source out moves the pooled BPD/AC/FL median by at most 2.9 mm. Siriraj sits slightly below the pool (about −0.3 to −0.6 SD) and Khon Kaen slightly above (+0.3 to +0.5 SD).
  • Not available online and not included: Siriraj HC (2000); Tongsong Chiang Mai BPD/HC/AC/FL (1990–91); Koranantakul Songkhla BPD/FL (1991); Chulalongkorn BPD/AC (1986, 1997).
  • Khon Kaen J Med Assoc Thai 2007;90:1963–9: Saksiriwuttho P, Ratanasiri T, Komwilaisak R. Fetal biometry charts for normal pregnant women in northeastern Thailand.

EFW formula

Default Auto: Hadlock-3 (HC, AC, FL) when HC and FL are entered, otherwise the Thai Tongsong 1992 formula (BPD, AC). In its own Thai derivation sample (162 babies) the Tongsong formula's mean absolute error (6.98%) was no better than Shepard's (6.89%), while Hadlock-3 is widely validated and was used in the 2026 Thai chart-comparison study.

Manual EFW: an EFW typed into the EFW box (for example the value your ultrasound machine reports) is used instead of the formula, for the centile, the chart and serial scans; it is marked "entered" and, in the serial table, with *. Whatever formula produced it, its centile is read from the selected EFW reference chart.

Single-centre references (selectable for comparison)

  • BPD (12–41 wk): Siwadune T, et al. Charts of Thai fetal biometries: 2. Biparietal diameter. J Med Assoc Thai 2000;83:292–8 (Siriraj, n=613). Table 2 50th centile and SD; centile = mean + z·SD.
  • FL (12–41 wk): Titapant V, et al. 3. Femur length. J Med Assoc Thai 2000;83:299–306 (Siriraj, n=608). Table 2 50th centile and SD.
  • HC (14–40 wk): Kankeow K. Charts of fetal biometries at Sukhothai Hospital. J Med Assoc Thai 2007;90:844–51 (rural northern Thailand, 151 women, 34 measurements per week, single operator). The published weekly means and SDs are uneven, so they are smoothed in the same way as the Siriraj charts: mean = −98.818 + 14.5231·GA + 0.019898·GA² − 0.002944·GA³ (within 3.7 mm of every weekly mean); SD = 9.048 − 0.04215·GA. This is not the Siriraj HC chart (Sutanthavibul 2000, Siriraj Hosp Gaz 52:445–51), which is not included. Sukhothai biometry runs lower than urban Thai charts near term.
  • AC (12–41 wk): Sunsaneevithayakul P, et al. 4. Abdominal circumference. J Med Assoc Thai 2000;83:307–14 (Siriraj). Modelled on the √AC scale; for each week √AC = m + s·z is fitted to the published 3rd/10th/50th/90th/97th centiles (the printed SD column does not reproduce them).
  • EFW chart (24–42 wk): Tongsong T, et al. J Med Assoc Thai 1993;76:482–6 (Chiang Mai), equations as given in Tongprasert F, et al. Obstet Gynecol Int 2026:6968622:
    mean = −1723.6688 + 59.37904·GA + 1.53150·GA² ; SD = 16.72198·GA − 327.80386
  • EFW chart, alternative (24–42 wk): Borisut P, Kovavisarach E. Standard intrauterine growth curve of Thai neonates delivered at Rajavithi Hospital. J Med Assoc Thai 2014;97:798–803 (Bangkok, 7,506 singleton births 2010–2013; GA confirmed by first-trimester or 14–20 wk ultrasound). Smoothed birthweight P5/P10/P50/P90 for all babies (Table 2) and by sex (Table 1), interpolated linearly between weeks. The paper gives no SD and no P3/P95/P97, so EFW is reported as a band (e.g. P5–P10) rather than an exact centile. In Table 1 the female P90 at 24–26 wk is above the male P90, an artefact of the paper's cubic smoothing at the extreme.
  • EFW formula (Thai): Tongsong T, Wanapirak C, Chaichonlatrap W. Thai J Obstet Gynaecol 1992;4:15–22: log₁₀EFW = 1.85479 + 0.09008·BPD + 0.02466·AC (cm).
  • Hadlock-3 (as printed in Tongprasert 2026): log₁₀EFW = 1.326 − 0.00326·AC·FL + 0.0107·HC + 0.0438·AC + 0.158·FL (cm).

Doppler references and FGR guidance

  • Umbilical artery PI, Thai (default) (12–41 wk): Sunsaneevithayakul P, Siwadune T, Boriboonhirunsarn D, Titapant V, Sutanthavibul A. Reference centile charts for fetal umbilical artery Doppler velocimetry waveform of Thai fetuses. Thai J Obstet Gynaecol 2000;12:201–11 (Siriraj, n=617). Mean = 1.081 − 0.015·GA + 11.23/GA; SD = 0.189 − 0.0026·GA. The paper prints the SD equation as “−0.189 − (0.0026/W)”, which gives a negative SD; the form used here reproduces its own SD column (0.16 at 12 wk to 0.09 at 40 wk) and its centile table within 0.01. Its SD is narrower than international charts, so more fetuses fall above the 95th centile (e.g. UA PI 1.15 at 35 wk: P99.7 Thai vs P94.1 INTERGROWTH).
  • Umbilical artery PI, international (24–40 wk): Drukker L, et al. International gestational age-specific centiles for umbilical artery Doppler indices: INTERGROWTH-21st. Am J Obstet Gynecol 2020;222:602.e1–15. Box–Cox equations (λ = −0.0768617); reproduces the paper’s worked example (PI 1.00 at 36+4 wk → z 0.87, P80.9).
  • MCA PI and cerebroplacental ratio (21–39 wk): Ebbing C, Rasmussen S, Kiserud T. Ultrasound Obstet Gynecol 2007;30:287–96 (Bergen, 161 low-risk pregnancies, 566 observations). No Thai CPR reference has been published; the Thai MCA PI study (Komwilaisak, Khon Kaen 2004) could not be obtained. Centiles use a two-piece log-normal fitted exactly to each week’s published 5th/50th/95th centiles (within 0.02 of all nine published centiles); 39+x wk uses the 39-wk row; no centile from 40+0 wk.
  • MCA peak systolic velocity: Mari G, et al. N Engl J Med 2000;342:9–14. Median = e2.31 + 0.046·GA cm/s; ≥1.5 MoM suggests moderate/severe fetal anaemia (not part of the FGR pathway).
  • Umbilical artery PI, serial (option) (19–41 wk): Acharya G, Wilsgaard T, Berntsen GKR, Maltau JM, Kiserud T. Reference ranges for serial measurements of umbilical artery Doppler indices in the second half of pregnancy. Am J Obstet Gynecol 2005;192:937–44 (Tromsø, 130 low-risk pregnancies, 513 observations, free loop). ln PI mean = 1.5075 − 0.2843·√GA; variance = 0.0667 + 0.00398·GA − 0.0276·√GA (Appendix); reproduces Table II within 0.01. Conditional (serial) ranges are not yet calculated.
  • Uterine artery mean PI (11–41 wk): Gómez O, Figueras F, Fernández S, Bennasar M, Martínez JM, Puerto B, Gratacós E. Reference ranges for uterine artery mean pulsatility index at 11–41 weeks of gestation. Ultrasound Obstet Gynecol 2008;32:128–32 (Barcelona, 620 women, mean of left and right PI; transvaginal at 11–14 wk, transabdominal from 15 wk). Centiles come from the published Table 2 (log-normal, interpolated between weeks). The printed equations have rounded coefficients and drift from the table by up to 0.017 at the 95th centile, so they are not used. The “>95th” yes/no box is used only when no PI is entered.
  • Ductus venosus PIV (21–39 wk): Kessler J, Rasmussen S, Hanson M, Kiserud T. Longitudinal reference ranges for ductus venosus flow velocities and waveform indices. Ultrasound Obstet Gynecol 2006;28:890–8 (Bergen, 160 low-risk pregnancies, 547 observations). The equations are in an online appendix that was not available, so centiles come from the published Table 4: a two-piece normal on the PIV scale fitted to each week’s 5th/50th/95th, interpolated between weeks (within 0.01 of all nine published centiles). As in ISUOG 2020, delivery timing uses the DV a-wave, not the PIV. The “>95th” yes/no box is used only when no PIV is entered or outside 21–39 wk.
  • FGR definition: Gordijn SJ, et al. Consensus definition of fetal growth restriction: a Delphi procedure. Ultrasound Obstet Gynecol 2016;48:333–9. Early FGR (<32 wk): AC/EFW <3rd or UA AEDF/REDF; or AC/EFW <10th with UtA PI >95th and/or UA PI >95th. Late FGR (≥32 wk): AC/EFW <3rd; or ≥2 of AC/EFW <10th, AC/EFW crossing >2 quartiles, CPR <5th or UA PI >95th. Centile crossing is taken from saved serial scans (largest fall of AC or EFW centile ≥50).
  • Management: Lees CC, et al. ISUOG Practice Guidelines: diagnosis and management of small-for-gestational-age fetus and fetal growth restriction. Ultrasound Obstet Gynecol 2020;56:298–312. Early FGR: deliver at 26+0–28+6 wk if DV a-wave at/below baseline or STV <2.6 ms; 29+0–31+6 wk if DV a-wave at/below baseline or STV <3.0 ms; ≥32+0 wk (permitted after 30+0) if UA REDF or STV <3.5 ms; ≥34+0 wk (permitted after 32+0) if UA AEDF or STV <4.5 ms; ≥26 wk if repeated unprovoked decelerations or BPP ≤4. Late FGR: deliver if decelerations, BPP ≤4, STV <3.5 ms (32+0–33+6) / <4.5 ms (≥34+0), or UA AEDF/REDF; at 36+0–37+0 wk if UA PI >95th; at 38+0 wk with cerebral redistribution. SGA: induce from 38+0 wk, not beyond 39+6 wk. The calculator also treats an abnormal DV a-wave after 32 wk as a delivery indication, because ISUOG lists DV only up to 31+6 wk while the UA criteria already apply from 32 wk.

Method notes

  • Fractional GA is interpolated linearly between weekly table rows. Percentile uses the exact normal CDF (Tongprasert 2026 used the logistic approximation 100/(1+e−1.7z); difference < 1 centile point).
  • 5th and 95th centiles are derived from the same mean and SD; the papers publish 3/10/50/90/97 only.
  • The printed BPD and FL regression equations use rounded coefficients that do not reproduce the papers’ own tables (e.g. BPD equation gives 78 mm at 40 wk vs 89 mm in Table 2), so the tables are used.
  • The BPD paper’s 97th centile at 40 wk repeats the 39-wk value (typo); this calculator computes 95.0 mm from P50 + 1.88·SD.
  • The EFW chart equation’s SD collapses toward zero below ~22 wk, so EFW centiles are given only from 24 wk.
  • Both EFW references are built from birthweights, not fetal ultrasound. Babies born preterm include more growth-restricted infants than fetuses of the same age that stay in the womb, so birthweight charts can sit low before about 37 weeks. At term the Rajavithi median is heavier than Chiang Mai (3,228 vs 3,102 g at 40 wk), so the choice of chart changes how many fetuses are flagged.
  • In the single-centre option, BPD, AC and FL come from the Siriraj cross-sectional series and HC from Sukhothai (longitudinal), so HC centiles are not strictly comparable with the others.
  • The pooled reference has not been validated against outcomes. Khon Kaen and Sukhothai excluded babies born SGA/LGA, which narrows their SDs; the between-centre term widens the pooled range. Pooling also mixes measurement eras (2000–2007 machines) and dating methods.
  • Serial scans are kept only in this browser on this device (local storage) and can be exported as CSV.
